# Privacy Policy — rtfm

_Last updated: 2026-06-28_

rtfm runs entirely on your local machine.

- **Data collected by the plugin author:** none. rtfm contains no telemetry,
  analytics, or tracking, and sends no data to the author or to any third party
  operated by the author.
- **Data processed locally:** rtfm reads the files you choose to index and stores
  an index in a local SQLite database inside your project (`.rtfm/`). This index
  and your content never leave your machine.
- **Third-party network access:** if you enable optional semantic search, rtfm
  downloads a sentence-embedding model from its model host (Hugging Face) on first
  use. No content from your project is transmitted during this download. rtfm makes
  no other network requests.

**Contact:** https://github.com/roomi-fields/rtfm/issues
